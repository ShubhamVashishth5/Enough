Make modes of your own in Settings > Modes: name one, start it from an existing mode and give it a schedule. A mode you turn on by hand now ends at a time you pick, then the schedules take over; a later end applies at once. Each Breathe app can have its own pause, from 5 seconds to 2 minutes. Typing a Vault app's name no longer opens another app that matches its first letters, and going back no longer shows one screen over another.
